Bamboo bike workshops · Catalonia · Spain

Build your own bamboo bicycle in Catalonia

We're preparing bamboo bicycle frame-building workshops in Sant Julià de Vilatorta, Osona — close to Vic and between Barcelona and Girona. A hands-on project bringing together bicycle design, frame geometry, natural materials and the riding landscape of Catalonia.

Coming soon — project preview. Workshop dates, places and commercial conditions have not been confirmed. The shop preview does not accept payments or real orders.

Bamboo bicycle frame-building workshops

Discover how bicycle geometry, frame tube selection and joining techniques come together in a hands-on build. The proposed workshops introduce frame construction and the practical realities of working with bamboo.

Location: Osona, near Barcelona and Girona

The proposed location is Sant Julià de Vilatorta in Osona, Catalonia. The region offers road and gravel rides around Vic, the Garrotxa, the Ripollès and the wider Girona area.

Frame Build Kits — DIY at home

For riders interested in a home build, we plan to present Bamboo Bicycle Club DIY frame-building kits. Availability, prices and final products will be confirmed before commercial launch.

Frame Materials and Bike Kits

Workshop frame-building materials are separate from bicycle component kits. Component kits can include wheels, cranks, bottom brackets, handlebars, drivetrains and brakes. The current cart is only a demonstration.
